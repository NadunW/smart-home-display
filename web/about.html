Home Smart Display

A wall display for the family. It shows everyone's appointments, tasks, reminders and the next bus on one screen in the kitchen, and the same information on everyone's phones.

Open Home Smart Display

What it does

  • Today and coming up: appointments for each family member, colour-coded by person, with a countdown to a big day.
  • Family tasks: daily and weekly chores, ticked off on the screen, with progress for each person.
  • Reminders that stay on the screen until someone ticks them off.
  • Reward notes for the kids: they earn notes for chores and habits and spend them on rewards.
  • Bus and trips: live departures from the nearest stop and saved trips (Västtrafik).
  • Weather for today and tomorrow.
  • Google Calendar (optional): each family member can connect their own Google account.

How Home Smart Display uses Google Calendar

A family member who chooses to connect their Google account gives Home Smart Display permission to:

  • See the list of their calendars (calendar.readonly), so they can choose which calendars show on the family display.
  • See, add, change and delete events in those calendars (calendar.events). Their appointments appear on the family display. Appointments and reminders that the family adds on the display for that person are put into the calendar they chose. Changes and deletions made on either side go to the other.

Only the event's title, date, time and length are used. They are shown only to the members of that family. Google data is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone else. Each person can disconnect at any time in Settings, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Home Smart Display's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who it is for

Home Smart Display is a private, non-commercial app made for one family's home. Family members sign in with their own account and join the family with a code. Everything a family adds is visible only to members of that family.
