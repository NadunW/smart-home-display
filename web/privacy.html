Privacy policy

Home Smart Display · Last updated 20 November 2026

Home Smart Display (“the app”) is a family wall display for appointments, tasks, reminders, reward notes, weather and bus times. It is a private, non-commercial app. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how you can have it deleted.

Information the app collects

  • Your account: your email address and a password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by the sign-in service.
  • What your family adds: the family name, the names and colours of the people on the display, and the appointments, tasks, reminders, reward notes, saved trips, bus stop and town that the family enters.
  • Google Calendar data, only if you connect your Google account:
    • your Google account's email address and account ID, so the display knows which account is connected;
    • the names and colours of your calendars, so you can choose which ones show;
    • for events from about two days ago to two months ahead in the calendars you choose: the title, date, start time, length and Google's event IDs;
    • an access token and a refresh token from Google, which let the app read and update those events for you.
  • Settings for each device (theme, layout, tab order) are kept only in that device's browser storage.

The app doesn't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

How the information is used

  • To show your family's information on the display and on your family members' phones.
  • Google Calendar: to show your appointments on your family's display, and to add, change or delete events in the calendar you chose when the family does that on the display. The app only does this for the calendars you pick in Settings.
  • Only members of your family, meaning people who signed in and joined with the family's code, can see your family's information. Nobody else can.

Google user data

Home Smart Display's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google Calendar data is used only to provide the calendar features described above. It is not used for anything else.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never transferred to anyone else. The exceptions are the hosting provider that stores it for the app, and cases where the law requires it.
  • No person reads it. The exceptions are you and your family on the display, or with your permission to fix a problem.
  • It is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Google tokens are stored on the server in a table that the app's web pages can't read. They are never sent to the browser.

Where it is stored and who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database, the sign-in and the server functions.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's web pages. It doesn't receive your family's data.
  • Google, if you connect Google Calendar: your events are read from and written to your Google account.
  • Västtrafik receives the bus stop and trip places you choose, to look up departures. It doesn't receive anything about you.
  • Open-Meteo receives the town's coordinates, to get the weather.

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Disconnect Google Calendar in Settings → Google Calendar → Disconnect. The app deletes your Google tokens and the appointments that came from your Google account, and withdraws its access at Google. Your Google Calendar itself isn't changed. You can also withdraw access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Anything you delete on the display is deleted from the database.
  • Synced Google events are kept only while they fall in the two-months-ahead window and your account stays connected.
  • To delete your account and your family's data, contact the app's owner (below). It is removed within 30 days.

Children

Children can appear on the display as names, for example on a reward chart, but they don't have accounts. Only a parent or guardian adds them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Significant changes are shown in the app.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/NadunW/smart-home-display, or ask the person who set up your family's display.
